Bustime Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. About Bustime

Bustime is an information-sharing app for searching bus routes, bus stops, route timings and bus-operator contact details. Users do not need to create an account or log in. Bustime does not itself take bookings, collect travel payments, or arrange transportation.

2. Information submitted by users and operators

  • Route submissions: route, stops, timings, optional service details, and a required mobile number. The number is used to contact the submitter and verify the information.
  • Bus operator listings: operator/company name, operating area/location, required contact number, available bus types and other service details. The administrator may call the operator and conduct field verification before approving a listing.
  • Feedback and deletion requests: a required mobile number and the message/details needed to understand, verify and respond to the request.

Submitting information does not guarantee publication. The administrator may review, approve or reject submissions, including submissions that appear invalid or spam-like.

3. How information is used

Submitted information is used to review and publish route or operator information, verify submissions, contact submitters, respond to feedback and deletion requests, prevent spam, and operate and protect the service. Operator contact numbers are displayed so users can contact the relevant operator directly. A route submitter's phone number is used for verification and is not intended to be displayed publicly with the route.

We do not sell users' submitted personal information. We do not use submitted phone numbers for unrelated advertising purposes.

4. Public listings and direct contact

Approved operator listings may show the operator/company name, operating area, available bus types and contact number. Operators are responsible for having permission to share the details they submit.

Bustime is not a booking or transport provider. Users contact operators directly. Availability, prices, passenger numbers, reservations, payments and travel arrangements are handled between the user and operator, outside Bustime. We review information before publication where applicable, but cannot guarantee that every listing, route, timing or phone number is always accurate or current. This does not exclude responsibilities that cannot legally be excluded.

5. Location and device information

Bustime does not provide a feature that tracks a user's live GPS location. An operator's area/location is collected for listing and verification, not to track the user's location. The app does not intentionally collect personal device details through a dedicated device-profiling feature.

6. App usage analytics

The current app code sends limited usage events to a Supabase backend, such as app opened, bus-route search and bus-stand search. These events may include a randomly generated session identifier stored on the device, app version, Android platform, and identifiers associated with the route or stand search. The analytics event payload is designed not to include the user's phone number, name or feedback message. These events are used to understand basic app usage and improve the service.

7. Service providers and third parties

Bustime uses GitHub for source-code hosting and Supabase for database/backend functions, including submitted information and the usage events described above. These providers may process technical information as needed to deliver and secure their services, subject to their terms and configurations.

Firebase, Google Analytics and Crashlytics are not currently described as integrated services. Google AdMob is planned but is not yet treated as active in this policy. Before AdMob is enabled in a release, this policy should be updated to describe the actual advertising, consent and data-processing settings. If enabled, Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP address or ad interactions as described in Google's applicable disclosures and settings.

8. Retention

Information is generally intended to be retained for approximately 90–120 days after review. Some information considered important for reliable route/service information, security, resolving disputes or complying with law may be retained longer where appropriate. Retention depends on the information type and applicable requirements.

9. Deletion requests

Users can submit a deletion request through the Delete Request option in Feedback. A mobile number and enough details to identify the information are required. We may contact the requester to verify the request. We will review valid requests and delete relevant information where appropriate, subject to legitimate operational, security or legal retention needs. Deletion may not be immediate.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information handled through the app and backend. No electronic system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children

Bustime does not impose a general minimum age of 18 for viewing transport information. The app does not require users to create accounts. Users should provide accurate information and avoid submitting unnecessary personal details. Any additional protections or consent required by applicable law for children's information still apply.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, backend services, advertising configuration or legal requirements change. The date above will be revised when material changes are made.

13. Contact and privacy requests

For questions, complaints, corrections or deletion requests, use the Feedback section inside Bustime. Describe the issue and provide a valid mobile number so the administrator can contact you for clarification.

14. Applicable law

This policy is intended to operate consistently with applicable laws. Nothing here removes rights or obligations that cannot legally be excluded.
