## English
BUSTIME — Important Notice & Terms

Bustime is a non-governmental information platform. It provides collected data about bus routes and timings. Timings, routes, and bus services may change, so please confirm the information before travelling.

Bustime does not guarantee any bus timing, route, service availability, or driver service and accepts no responsibility in relation to them.

The sole purpose of Bustime is to provide information, not to guarantee any service.
