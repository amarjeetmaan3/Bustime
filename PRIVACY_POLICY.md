# Bustime Privacy Policy
**Last updated: 24 November 2026**

## 1. About Bustime
Bustime is an information-sharing app for searching bus routes, bus stops, route timings and bus-operator contact details. It is developed and operated by Amarjeet Maan, an independent developer in India (“we”, “us”). Users do not need to create an account or log in.

Bustime is an information service only. It is not a bus operator, ticket seller or booking service, and it does not take bookings, collect travel payments or arrange transportation.

## 2. Information we collect
**a) Information you submit** (only if you use these features):

- **Route submissions and corrections:** route, stops, timings, optional service details and notes, and a required mobile number. The number is used to contact the submitter and verify the information.
- **Bus operator listings:** operator/company name, operating area, contact and WhatsApp number, bus types and other service details. The administrator may call the operator to verify before approving a listing.
- **Feedback and data-deletion requests:** a required mobile number and the message needed to understand and answer the request.

**b) Information collected automatically**

- **Usage events:** app opened, first launch of an installation, bus-route search and bus-stand search, with a randomly generated identifier stored on the device, the app version, the Android platform and the route/stand identifiers searched. These events do not contain your phone number, name or message. Reinstalling the app or clearing its data creates a new random identifier and may be counted as a new installation.
- **Data-update diagnostics:** when the app downloads route updates, it may send a random device identifier, app version, the latest data revision it has, and, if an update fails, a short technical error message. This is used only to find and fix data-update problems.
- **Advertising data** collected by Google AdMob (see section 5).

**c) What we do not collect:** we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, files, messages, or call logs. An operator’s “area” is collected only for the listing, not to track a user.

## 3. How we use information
To review and publish route and operator information, verify submissions, contact submitters, answer feedback and deletion requests, prevent spam and abuse, fix data-update problems, understand basic app usage, show ads, and operate and protect the service. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use submitted phone numbers for advertising.

## 4. Public listings, accuracy and no guarantee
Approved operator listings may show the operator/company name, operating area, bus types and contact number so that users can contact the operator directly. Operators must have permission to share the details they submit. A route submitter’s phone number is used only for verification and is not shown with the route.

The information in Bustime is collected from different sources and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Bus timings, routes and services can change, run late, be cancelled, or stop for a day, a few days or permanently. Bustime does not guarantee any bus, timing, route, service or contact number, and is not responsible for any bus service, driver, conductor or operator. Please confirm with the operator before travelling. Nothing here excludes responsibility that cannot legally be excluded.

## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob)
Bustime shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) in these formats: banner, native, full-screen (interstitial) and app-open ads. Ads can be switched on or off and scheduled by the administrator. When an ad is requested, Google and its partners may process the device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions such as impressions and clicks, to deliver, limit the frequency of, measure and protect ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Bustime asks for your choice through the Google consent form before any ad is requested.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings under Google → Ads. More information: [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Bustime’s own sponsor/poster messages are managed separately by the administrator and are not part of AdMob.

## 6. Service providers
We use **Supabase** for the database and backend functions that store submissions, usage events and data-update diagnostics, and **Google AdMob** for ads. This policy page is a static web page that may log ordinary web-request information when opened. These providers process data on our behalf or under their own terms, and data may be stored or processed on servers outside India.

## 7. How long we keep information
**Feedback and data-deletion requests** are deleted automatically by a scheduled job that runs every day: 90 days after the administrator reviews them (180 days after they were sent if never reviewed). Other information is kept as follows:

| Information | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Route submissions, route corrections and bus-operator submissions (including the mobile number sent with them) | Kept with the record of the change, so that the published route or listing can be verified and kept accurate. We remove your number or the record on request (see section 8). |
| Usage events and data-update diagnostics (random identifier, app version, error messages) | Kept for as long as needed to understand app usage and keep data updates working, and may be cleaned up periodically. |
| Published routes, stops and operator listings | Kept while they are active, until the operator asks for removal or the administrator removes them. |
| Advertising data | Held by Google under its own policies. |

Short-term backups kept by our service provider may hold deleted data for a limited time. Information may be kept longer where needed to resolve a dispute, protect against abuse, or comply with law.

## 8. Your choices and deletion requests
You may ask us to correct or delete the information you submitted, or to remove your number from a published operator listing. Use **Feedback → Delete my data** in the app (a mobile number and enough details to identify the information are required), or email [support@bustime.example](mailto:support@bustime.example). We may contact you to verify the request. We aim to reply within 30 days. Because there are no user accounts in Bustime, there is no account to close.

## 9. Security
Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, public tables only accept new submissions, and review and publishing tools are restricted to the administrator. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 10. Children
Bustime is a general public transport information app, designed for people aged 13 and over, and is not directed to children under 13. Searching routes and timings does not need any personal information. The forms that ask for a mobile number (route and correction submissions, operator listings, feedback) are meant for adults aged 18 or over; if you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to send it for you. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes
We may update this policy when the app, backend services, advertising or the law change. The date above will be revised, and material changes will also be shown in the app.
